# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Churn Rate | Churned customers divided by customers in the measurement population |
| Retention Rate | Customers retained divided by customers eligible for retention measurement |
| Cohort Retention | Share of a signup/enrollment cohort remaining active at a defined interval |
| DAU | Distinct active users on a day |
| MAU | Distinct active users in a month |
| Course Completion Rate | Completed course enrollments divided by eligible course enrollments |
| Average Session Duration | Mean session duration for the selected population |
| ARPU | Revenue divided by customers in the selected period |
| LTV | Estimated customer lifetime value based on documented project assumptions |
| Renewal Rate | Renewed subscriptions divided by subscriptions eligible for renewal |

All calculations will be implemented consistently across SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI.
