# Business Recommendations

The recommendations are hypotheses generated from the synthetic analysis and should be validated with real customer data before implementation.

- Trigger re-engagement journeys when activity and session duration decline.
- Prioritize incomplete-course users for personalized learning paths and reminders.
- Use weekly progress reporting and milestone/gamification nudges for low-engagement cohorts.
- Test flexible plans and completion-linked incentives for price-sensitive users.
- Route repeated support issues to faster-resolution or mentor-assisted workflows.
- Run controlled A/B tests and measure retention, completion, engagement and revenue impact.
