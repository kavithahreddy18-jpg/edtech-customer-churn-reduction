# Business Analysis

## Business Problem

The case study examines customer churn in a B2C EdTech platform. The analysis focuses on learner engagement, course completion, lifecycle drop-off, pricing/subscription behaviour, feedback and customer experience.

## Stakeholder View

- Product team: improve learning experience and course engagement
- Customer success/support: identify at-risk learners and intervene earlier
- Marketing/CRM: target retention campaigns by segment
- Finance/Revenue: understand renewal, ARPU and LTV
- Leadership: monitor churn, retention and intervention impact

## User Journey

Acquisition → Signup → Onboarding → Course Enrollment → Learning Activity → Course Completion → Subscription Renewal / Drop-off

## Key Questions

1. What are the primary churn triggers across user segments?
2. At what lifecycle stage does churn peak?
3. Which engagement metrics are associated with retention?
4. How does pricing relate to churn behaviour?
5. What interventions can reduce churn?

## Analysis-to-Action Framework

Finding → Root cause → Affected segment → Intervention → KPI → Experiment → Outcome measurement

The project will avoid presenting recommendations as facts until they are supported by analysis of the generated dataset.
