# Problem Statement

The EdTech business is experiencing customer churn associated with low course completion, declining engagement after onboarding, expectation/content mismatch, pricing sensitivity, and perceived low ROI. The analysis focuses on identifying measurable churn drivers, locating lifecycle drop-off points, segmenting customers by behaviour and value, and translating findings into retention interventions.

This portfolio uses a **synthetic but realistic dataset** created for demonstration and interview purposes. It is not company-confidential or production data.
